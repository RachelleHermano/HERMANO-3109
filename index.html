Product Description

Compared to wired headphones, there is little difference in sound quality with wireless headphones; wireless ones are more convenient, mainly because the wire in the middle is replaced by radio waves. Wireless headphones can be divided into three parts: the first part is the sound source, the second part is the receiver, and the third part is the headphone unit. The function of this part is primarily to convert the signals transmitted from a mobile phone or receiver into sound and then transmit it to the human ear.
